About me

I'm a Computer Science student at Iowa State University with a soft spot for front-end craft and the unglamorous backend work that makes it reliable. Over the past three years I've moved from tutorial-following to shipping small tools that real people use — a study-group scheduler, a course-review database, a couple of Chrome extensions that scratched my own itch.

I work best when a project has a genuine constraint to solve — a deadline, a data quirk, a user who complains when a button is one pixel off. Outside of coursework, I mentor incoming CS majors through my department's peer program and spend an unreasonable amount of time reorganizing my Obsidian notes.

I'm looking for a summer 2027 internship where I can write production code, get told when it's wrong, and get better at it.
