About me

I'm an Entrepreneurship major at Iowa State University on the pre-law track. I'm drawn to the overlap between the two — building something from scratch teaches you exactly where contracts, negotiation, and regulation actually matter, instead of just reading about them in a casebook.

I co-founded a small campus venture during my sophomore year, which meant learning to draft agreements, negotiate with vendors, and occasionally argue our way out of a bad lease clause — the parts of business that made me want to go to law school in the first place. Outside of class, I compete on the mock trial team and serve as a chapter officer for our entrepreneurship club.

I'm looking for a summer 2027 internship or pre-law fellowship where I can work at the intersection of business strategy and legal practice — think transactional law, business development, or a startup legal team.
